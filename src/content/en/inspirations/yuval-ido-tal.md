Yuval Ido Tal has practiced meditation for close to three decades, and
has taught Buddhism and Zen for more than 25 years. Between 2010 and
2020 he served as academic director of the Psychodharma school, and
throughout his years there he developed and taught across its various
programs — from introductory meditation courses to multi-year training
programs for therapists.

Beyond teaching, Yuval is a writer, poet, researcher, and wanderer. He
wrote the books *Buddhism: A Short Introduction* and the poetry
collection *My Heart Was Startled by the Moon*, and translated and
published a selection *From the Poems of Ogawa Yukimitsu*. Alongside
his own writing, he translates traditional texts and books on
consciousness, philosophy, meditation, and the arts of Buddhism and
Zen — work that bridges scholarly depth with accessible language,
without diluting the source.

Yuval currently lives between Kyoto and Kfar Saba, and alongside
teaching also practices the art of calligraphy. You can hear him
share this perspective, on Buddhism and on practice, in episode 36 of
Psychodharma's [Hakarit podcast](https://www.pdharma.com/hakarit-podcast/episode-36).
